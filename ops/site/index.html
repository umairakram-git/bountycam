BountyCam

Ask the real world. Someone nearby verifies it.

Need something checked somewhere else?

Post a Bounty: a paid question about a real place. Is the EV charger working? Is the product on the shelf? Has the work been done? A Scout nearby goes there, captures the photos you asked for inside the app, and submits them.

How it works

  1. Ask. Describe the place and the photos you need, and set a reward.
  2. Fund. The reward is locked in a Solana escrow, so Scouts know the job is paid for.
  3. Verify. A Scout accepts, goes to the spot and captures live photos in the app.
  4. Settle. You approve and the Scout is paid, or you name the requirement that was not met and an arbiter decides.

Each submission is anchored on chain with a cryptographic receipt. The photos themselves stay private between you and the Scout.

The video plays from YouTube when you press play. Open it on YouTube

Devnet release. This version runs on Solana devnet with test USDC. No real money moves.
