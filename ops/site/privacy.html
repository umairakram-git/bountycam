Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what the BountyCam app and the service at bountycam.app, operated by a solo developer in Australia ("we"), collect and why.

What we collect

  • Wallet address. Your public Solana address, used to sign you in and to settle bounties.
  • Bounties you create. Title, category, the location you choose, photo prompts, reward and timings.
  • Mission evidence. When you capture evidence as a Scout: the photos, the time of capture, and your device's location at capture, used to check you were at the mission location.
  • Seeker verification. If you verify a Seeker device, a record that your wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token.
  • Server logs. Request paths and times, kept for operating the service.

We do not track your location in the background, show ads, or sell personal data.

Who can see what

  • Photos are stored privately. Only the requester of the bounty and BountyCam's arbiter (when a dispute is opened) can view them.
  • Before a Scout accepts, only an approximate distance to a bounty is shown; the exact spot is shown after acceptance.
  • The requester never sees a Scout's live location.
  • On chain, a bounty's escrow records wallet addresses, amounts, states and a cryptographic hash of the evidence. Blockchain records are public and permanent; they never contain the photos or locations themselves.

Where data is stored

On a server in Sydney, Australia (OVHcloud), and on the Solana blockchain as described above.

How long

BountyCam is a test release on Solana devnet; data may be deleted when the test environment is reset. You can ask us to delete your off-chain data at any time.

Your choices

Camera and location permissions are requested only when needed and can be withdrawn in your device settings. Contact us to access or delete your data.
